# Beauty and the Beast

Main Character:
1. Belle	Paige 
2. Beast/Prince

Beauty and the Beast follows Belle, a bright young woman who becomes a captive in the castle of a mysterious Beast, where enchanted servants help her see beyond appearances. The film blends romance, humor, and lavish animation, featuring memorable songs by Alan Menken and Howard Ashman. Praised for its story, characters, and groundbreaking visuals, it became the first animated film nominated for Best Picture and is regarded as a Disney classic worth watching.
